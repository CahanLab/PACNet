## About PACNet

Platform-Agnostic CellNet (PACNet) is the newest version of [CellNet](https://github.com/CahanLab/CellNet), a network-biology platform that assesses the fidelity of cellular engineering and generates hypotheses for improving cell derivations. Unlike CellNet, PACNet works across transcriptome profiling and expression-estimation methods, and it does not require complete gene overlap between query and reference samples. That makes it possible to compare cell-fate engineering protocols across studies.

The web app includes panels of engineered samples from state-of-the-field protocols for HSPC, heart, intestine/colon, liver, lung, neuron and skeletal muscle, so you can benchmark your cells against the most commonly used protocols without repeating those experiments. The web app analyzes human data; resources for applying PACNet to human and mouse data in R are in the [PACNet repository](https://github.com/CahanLab/PACNet).
